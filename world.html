The World

A realm of five territories, waking into danger

Clans spread across a shared hex map, each terrain color feeding a different resource. They build, trade, and grow — while a buried Shadow Amulet counts down toward something worse.

The Shadow Amulet

Buried somewhere in the realm, the Amulet doesn't stay quiet. As fragments of it awaken over the course of a game, it spawns monsters, empowers the ones already loose, and corrupts the terrain itself — each awakening tied directly to the clans' own progress, as if the realm is answering how fast they build.

The escalation ladder

  • Round 3 — first fragment stirs. Monsters begin testing the camps.
  • Round 6 — second fragment wakes. Pressure increases across the board.
  • Round 9 — third fragment wakes. The realm is openly hostile now.
  • Round 12 — final fragment wakes, and the Boss Monster arrives — a fight no single clan can win alone.

Four tiers of threat

Not every monster the Amulet calls is equal. The sixteen cataloged so far split into four tiers by danger — see the full roster on the Monsters page.
