The World

A realm of six terrains, waking into danger

Clans spread across a shared hex map, each terrain feeding the one resource it actually produces. They build, trade, and grow — while the Shadow Amulet counts down toward something worse.

The Shadow Amulet

The Amulet doesn't stay quiet. As each fragment of it wakes, it tears a rift open to the north, and monsters pour through — empowering the ones already loose and corrupting the terrain they cross. Each awakening is tied directly to the clans' own progress, as if the realm is answering how fast they build.

The escalation ladder

  • Round 3 — first fragment stirs. Monsters begin testing the camps.
  • Round 6 — second fragment wakes. Pressure increases across the board.
  • Round 9 — third fragment wakes. The realm is openly hostile now.
  • Round 12 — final fragment wakes, and the Boss Monster arrives — a fight no single clan can win alone.
