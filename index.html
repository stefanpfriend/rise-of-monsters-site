Where this comes from

A prototype five years in the making

Rise of Monsters started as a tabletop concept in 2020 and has been rebuilt, rule by rule, into a hex-map resource game with cooperative monster combat layered on top. The art, cards, and rules you'll find here are pulled straight from the source material — nothing here is invented to fill space. The browser prototype is where we're pressure-testing it before it's ready for a table.
